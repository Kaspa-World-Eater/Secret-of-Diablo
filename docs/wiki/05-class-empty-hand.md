# The Empty Hand

*Class doc. Code id `monk`. Shown in game as **The Empty Hand**. Lore rewritten 2026-09-29, third pass.*

**The user's direction for this pass:**
- The original order lore was 90% right; keep it and deepen it.
- **Radiance is erasing light.**
- He is **just a monk of the same order**, not the monk of legend, and nothing odd about his body.
- **The lore is about the order, not the man.**
- The man is a beggar. Or is he? Keep him a mystery.

**Still gone:** the mask (in any form), the fat gilded Buddha, the Weight meter and the yin-yang orb.

*Wiki hub: `wiki/00-start-here.md`.*

> "Put a name in the bowl."

## 1. The order at a glance

- **The Order of the Gilded Peak, the Kinrei-shū**, "the Gold Bell". Their monastery stood on a mountain of the god's knee-bone, high above the lowlands.

- **They serve Ur-Nihl, the Silence:** the thing that unsaid the god. It has no name, only negations.

- Most people say **the Hush**, and the Reading says **the Void That Emanates**. Only the Peak's monks and the mad say "Ur-Nihl" plainly.

- **What the lowlands saw:** gaunt, gentle monks who chanted night and day to hold the miasma off the slopes, and came down to beg.

- **What the Peak believed:** the Silence is not the god's murderer. It is the god's **rest**, and every living thing in a world made of a dying god is a wound that has not yet been allowed to close.

- **Non-duality** is implied only; there is no literal yin-yang anywhere:

- laughing and weeping

- gold and dark

- blow and blessing

- self and other

## 2. The two hands

The Peak taught that **the Silence has two hands, and both are empty.**
- **Radiance, the open hand, erases with light.**
  - It is noon with no shadow: white fire, glare, a brightness so complete it leaves nothing to see.
  - The Peak's gold was for this. Gold leaf in full sun gives back so much light that the face beneath it disappears.
  - *To be looked at and not seen* was the Peak's word for holiness.
- **Absence, the closed hand, erases with dark:** the palm that swallows, the pinch, the gate that closes over the sun.
- **Neither hand keeps anything.** That is why the lowlands called every monk of the Peak **an Empty Hand**, long before it meant any one of them.

## 3. What the world says (fragments)

**Begging Bowl of the Peak**

> *A plain wooden bowl, worn black at the lip.*
> *The monks of the Gilded Peak never begged for food. They held out the bowl and asked, "Put a name in it."*
> *The lowlanders learned to whisper into it the names they wanted gone: a grief, a debt, a man. The monks carried the names up the mountain, and the lowlands forgot them. Gratefully, mostly.*

**Gilded Leaf**

> *Beaten gold, thin as breath, from the halls of the Gilded Peak.*
> *The Peak gilded everything that mattered: bells, lamps, the faces of its saints, until nothing could be seen of them but light.*
> *The monks called it mercy. The pilgrims called it frightening. Both were right.*

**Black-Flame Lantern**

> *A lamp of the Peak. Its flame is black, and throws light anyway.*
> *Every soul the lanterns of the Hide burn chose to be kept. The Peak's souls chose otherwise: they asked to be unsaid slowly, and burn their own names for fuel.*
> *When the name is all gone, the lamp goes out. The Peak's monks carried their teachers this way, and walked more carefully than anyone.*

**The Chant of One Breath**

> *The Peak chanted in single breaths: one long syllable, then silence, then another.*
> *The lowlands thought the syllables held the miasma back. The novices were taught that the syllables were only there to mark the silences, and that the silences were the prayer.*
> *No novice was allowed to listen to the silence alone.*

**Weeping Saint, gilded**

> *A statue from the inner sanctum, face lost under gold. Dark tracks run from its eyes down the leaf.*
> *Pilgrims said the saints wept for the living. The monks never corrected them.*

**Inscription on the sanctum door** (scratched after the fall)

> *THEY WERE NOT WEEPING FOR US*

**Courtyard Glass**

> *Smooth black glass from the courtyard of the Gilded Peak, which was flagstone until the night the dead came up the stair.*
> *There are shapes inside it, very faint, all turned toward the sanctum door.*

**Words of a beggar-monk** (his rare lines)

> *"Put a name in the bowl."*
> *"Light is only the fastest way to forget."*
> *"I am no one's teacher. Ask the lamp."*

## 4. The Peak as it lived

**The practice.** Monks of the Peak fasted to lightness, slept little and owned nothing but a bowl, a robe and in time a lantern.
- A monk's whole labour was to empty himself enough that the Silence could pass through him without harming anything it didn't mean to.
- The miasma is breath gone wrong. **The chant held it off the slopes** because a monk who has truly emptied himself breathes right, and wrong breath cannot stand near him.

**The gold.** Every holy thing on the Peak was gilded: bells, lamps, the saints, eventually the faces of the old masters when they died.
- The gold was never wealth. **It was Radiance made solid**, a way to erase a face without destroying it.
- **The inner sanctum** was lined with gilded saints, and they wept.

**The begging.** Monks went down to the lowlands with bowls. They never asked for food. They asked for names: the sorrows, shames and dead the lowlanders wanted gone.
- The names were carried up the mountain and given to the Silence, and the lowlands forgot.
- It was the Peak's mercy, and the reason the lowlands fed the monks anyway, and feared them.

**The Destroyer's stone.** The Peak was cut from the god's knee-bone, and its oldest masters knew how to make the mountain move: bars, grips, pagodas and a weeping stone guardian at the gate. This is the third tree, paid in the body's own poise.

**The fall.** The **Pale Order**, the Ossuarch's order, learned that the Peak had been gilding its dead and giving them to the Silence, leaving no bone.
- To the Order that was the worst waste in the world. Its dead came up the Peak's stair to take the bones before the Hush could (see `claude/godmarrow-class-ossuarch.md`).
- The monks were too light to fight and too gentle to try, so each was cut down and raised before he hit the ground.
- **What happened next is not agreed.** By morning the courtyard was black glass. The dead were gone, and the doors of the inner sanctum were open.

## 5. What the Peak hid (for us; never stated plainly in game)

- **The saints were not statues.** They were the Peak's old masters, who went so far into the practice that there was nothing left of them for the Silence to take.
   - The gold was put on them while they still breathed, to hold their shape, because nothing else would.
   - **They are still alive under it.** The gold keeps them from finishing.
   - They were weeping because they had been kept, not let go. *They were not weeping for us.*

- **The chant did not hold back the miasma.** The miasma kept away because the Silence was so close to the Peak that breath itself went quiet there. The chant was to keep the novices from noticing how close.

- **The names went somewhere.** Every name carried up in a bowl and given to the Silence was gone from the Hide. The Peak never asked whether the Silence was only forgetting them, or keeping them.

## 6. The man

*The lore is about the order. About him, there is very little.*
- **What is seen:** a barefoot beggar-monk with a wooden bowl, a vermilion shawl and a lantern that burns black. He comes to villages at dusk and asks for a name.
- **What is known:** things he strikes leave smooth black glass, a little dust, or nothing, never a corpse. That is the Silence's signature.
- **What is said:**
  - He is one of the Peak's monks, one who was down in the lowlands with his bowl the night the Peak fell.
  - He is the one who opened the sanctum doors.
  - When the Order's knights came back to the Peak to count, there was a gilded saint missing from the sanctum.
  - There is no gold on him. There never was. He is a beggar, and that is all.
- **What he says about it:** "Put a name in the bowl."
- **For us:** he may be a monk, a saint with the gold worn off, or older than the Peak. He may be immortal. Leave every door open.
- **Other names:** **the Smiling Oblivion**, **Hōkai-bō** ("the Monk of the Broken Realm", an old pilgrim name for any monk of the Peak), and **the Palm** in the flesh-houses, where they spit when they say it.

## 7. How he plays (and why)

### The hourglass: the two hands in one glass

- **Radiance** pours amber sand *down* into the lower bulb.

- **Absence** pours black sand *up* into the upper bulb, falling the wrong way.

- **Destroyer** costs poise instead (0.6 × the listed cost).

**The fuller a bulb, the weaker that tree:** strength = 1 − 0.9 × f^2.5, gentle at first, then steep. Half full is still 84%; full is 10%.

**What a cast pours:** 5% of a bulb for a skill of the common cost, more for dear ones, never more than 25% at once. Turning the sky pours three times over. Nothing ever locks him out.

**The sand always runs back:**
- 10% a second while he fights
- 35% a second once he has stopped casting for 0.4 s
- both bulbs 1.5% on every hit and 12% on every kill
- a quarter of both when he drinks from the bowl

**No waits:** the dear skills cost more sand instead.

**Code:** `zz_monk_sand.js`.
- `P.kR` and `P.kA` are the two sands.
- `P.mana` is kept as the room left in the glass.
- `KS.sand(tab)` is a tree's strength.

### The Wheel of the Sky

By day Radiance is stronger; by night Absence is. Underground counts as dusk.
- **Halo-That-Turns** forces noon for 20 s.
- **Gate-Without-A-Gate** forces night for 20 s.

### His melee is everyone's

His three-blow string, the held heavy and the roll are the model for every order (`zz_melee_chain.js`).

**The Black-Flame Road: the channels of the Empty Hand.**
- Major Arcana sit over the lower dantian.
- Minor roads run along the two midlines, the meridians and the spring in the sole.
- **Notables:** the Hundred Meetings, the Sea of Breath, Lantern in the Fist, the Empty Laugh, the Bubbling Spring, the Barefoot Gate, Three Miles More.

## 9. Look (the character as he is)

- **Source:** the approved HD beggar-monk, baked in `show64/px_monk` and in the game since v0.53 (`zz_hero_monk_hd.js`). The user's reference standard is `refs/monk_std`.

- **Body:** gaunt, old and bald.

- **Head:** a long, matted grey beard.

- **Clothes:** a dried-blood vermilion shawl, the one bold mass, over an ash-grey robe; a rope belt, beads and small brass bells.

- **Feet:** bare, always.

- **Carried:** the wooden bowl, and the black-flame lantern floating a little behind (code).

- **No mask, ever.**

- **Scale:** one art pixel per screen pixel (168 art px tall); the world around him is drawn a third larger.

- **Moves:** idle, walk and a three-blow chain; cast, flurry, sky fist, sky, clap, hungry palm, pinch; heavy, leap, dodge, hit and death.

## 10. The order's enemies

- **The Pale Order** (the Ossuarch's order): the bloodiest feud. The Order's dead took the Peak, and something on the Peak took them back. The Peak leaves glass where the Order wants bone.

- **The flesh-houses:** the Peak starved the flesh; the flesh-houses feast. They hate the Silence more than they hated the Peak.

- **The Hollow Mystic's lodges:** to them the Silence is a weave with no pattern left, and they bar their doors to anyone who speaks its name.

- **The Shrine Keepers:** they set spirits back in their places. The Peak would rather have quieted them altogether, and the Keepers hunt its monks for it.

## 11. Writing rules for him

- **Nouns:** bowl, name, gold, leaf, glare, noon, lantern, bell, breath, syllable, silence, glass, stair, sanctum, saint, palm.

- **Verbs:** erase, forget, gild, give, close, open, ask.

- **Never:**

- the mask

- anything about his own body or fasting

- making him the legend

- explaining the saints

- a literal yin-yang

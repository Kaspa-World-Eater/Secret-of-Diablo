# The Errant Ways (archetypes, proposal 2026-09-30)

Artifact: https://claude.ai/artifact/RFUbPmbnSKshapF4EwbWvt

Every order keeps a rule; an Errant broke exactly one. Three per order, chosen once per pilgrim at the order's Broken Shrine in Act II (proposal). Each gives a fourth skill page (three Errant skills plus perks) and changes one rule of the order.

## The Hollow Mystic
Yh'Anuul, the Veiled Crone · draws Essence · trees: Mirror, Soul, Thread

The rule: The lodge turns, reads the thread, and keeps the dead it is given.

### The Anchorite of Glass
*Stands still, and lets the mirrors walk.*

> **Anchorhold.** An anchorhold of polished iron, one pace square, its inside a single mirror. The anchorites of the Polished Heart were walled into these and fed through a slot. They did not starve. When the lodge fell, one was found open from the inside, empty, and still polished.

- Rule broken: The lodge turns. He stops turning.
- Plays: A fortress caster. While he stands his ground, his Standing Mirrors never fade, and every spell he casts is cast again from each mirror with a clear line to its target. A step shatters the farthest mirror. Positioning is the whole game: he walks to the right place, raises his glass, and holds it.
- Loop: walk in → raise glass → stand → spells repeat from every mirror → step only when a mirror must move
- Skills: **The Anchorhold**: Mirrors rise in a ring around him. Anything caught inside sees only itself and strikes its own reflection. · **Angle of Return**: Passive. His needles and wisps glance off standing glass, and each glance adds weight to the blow. · **Walled In**: Passive. Each moment he stands unmoved adds to his armour and to the weight of his spells. The glass around him visibly thickens.
- Gains: Spells repeat from every mirror; Mirrors never fade while he stands; Much harder to hurt in place. Gives up: Walking breaks his glass; The Iron Golem only carries one mirror into the fight; Slow to reposition.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 4/1/2/2. Cousin: a turret build, like PoE totems, with no totems.

### The Needle-Brother
*Sews the crowd into one body, then cuts the thread.*

> **Needle in a Doorframe.** Mourners paid the Needle-Brothers to sew their dead a little longer into the world. A few took payment the other way, from the living, to sew someone out. The lodge struck their names from the circle. Their needles are still found in doorframes with the thread still through the eye.

- Rule broken: The lodge reads threads. He ties them.
- Plays: A pack wrangler at middle range. Threads run between the creatures he strikes. Whatever lands on one is shared down its threads to the rest. Pull the seam tight to drag the whole knot together, then cut every thread at once. Each severed thread bursts for what flowed through it. His wisps stop diving and sew instead.
- Loop: stitch a line through the pack → share every blow down the seam → draw the knot → unpick it all at once
- Skills: **Running Stitch**: A needle passes through up to five creatures and sews them into one seam. · **The Knot**: Hold. The seam draws tight and pulls everything sewn into it toward its middle. · **Unpicking**: Every thread is cut. Each bursts for a share of all the harm that ran along it.
- Gains: Blows shared across a sewn pack; Wisps sew threads on every strike; Huge single release. Gives up: Weak against lone foes; Mirror walls are thinner; A slow start in every fight.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 3/3/2/3. Cousin: Diablo II's Amplify Damage and Corpse Explosion in one loop.

### The Unwoven
*Walks where the pattern shows through.*

> **Page Cut from the Pir's Book.** Hold a lamp to him and look in the mirror behind him. The room is there, and the lamp. The Pir wrote that the Hollow is the space inside the weave where the pattern shows. She did not write that anyone could stand in it. That page was cut from her book.

- Rule broken: A soul cannot pass its own reflection. He has none.
- Plays: A close-range skirmisher who passes through what he fights. Wraith Form becomes his stance. Every creature he walks through is threaded and gives up Essence to him, and the choir follows in his wake and dives into whatever he threaded. He weaves back and forth through a pack like a shuttle through a loom. Standing still is how he dies.
- Loop: pass through a line → thread each one → turn → pass back → the choir dives the threads
- Skills: **Through**: A dash through every creature in a line, threading each one. · **Shuttle**: Hold. He runs back and forth along his last line, and every pass pulls the threads tighter. · **The Empty Space**: While he stands inside a creature's outline, that creature cannot strike him. His outline thins so you can see it.
- Gains: Essence from every pass; Wisps strike threaded foes; Hard to pin down. Gives up: No Iron Golem: the coat hangs empty; Fragile once he stops; Needs room to move.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 1/5/1/4. Cousin: a phasing skirmisher like PoE's Whirling Blades, done through bodies.

## The Ossuarch
Oss-Vharoth, the Bearing Mother · draws Marrow · trees: Ossuary, Carapace, the Count

The rule: The dead walk ahead, the count is kept in the book, and the warden stays on the stair.

### The Bearer
*Carries the dead instead of leading them.*

> **The Alcove on the Nine Stairs.** In the Ossuary of Nine Stairs there is an alcove shaped like a man, and the bones in its walls are set facing inward. The novices are told it is where the first warden rested. The sweepers say it is not empty. They say it has never been empty, only lighter in some years than others.

- Rule broken: The dead walk ahead. He takes them up instead.
- Plays: A walking fortress. Each skeleton he raises climbs into his frame, up to nine. Every one he carries adds reach, poise and life, and costs him speed. A heavy blow shakes one loose; it fights until it climbs back. With nine aboard he cannot be staggered, and his melee sweeps a great arc.
- Loop: raise → the dead climb aboard → walk slowly into the crowd → sweep → set them down when he must
- Skills: **Take Up**: A skeleton climbs into his frame. You see it: another figure fused into his plate. · **The Weight**: Passive. Each one carried adds reach, poise and life, and takes away speed. · **Set Down**: All he carries step out at once in a ring and strike. For a while he is light and quick.
- Gains: Nine carried: never staggered; Longest melee reach in the game; Takes blows for a whole army. Gives up: The slowest walker in the game; No free-standing army; Ranged foes can dance around him.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 1/1/3/1. Cousin: a Juggernaut who wears his army.

### The Tallier
*Counts to nine, and nothing is left.*

> **A Tallier's Cord.** The Talliers carry open counts in their heads until they close. Some counts do not close. There is a Tallier in the lowlands who has been counting one man for eleven years. The man is still alive. He has begun to walk with a limp, and does not know why.

- Rule broken: The count is kept in the book. He keeps it on the living.
- Plays: A rhythm duellist. Every blow cuts a notch, and blows come in threes. When a count closes at nine, one notch carries to every creature within reach, so one closed count can start a chain that closes a whole pack. The sigils over their heads show the count, and he plays to the beat.
- Loop: strike in threes → notches climb on the sigils → close at nine → the notch carries → the next count closes sooner
- Skills: **Cut the Notch**: His three-blow string. The third blow in each string cuts two notches. · **Carry the Nine**: Passive. A closed count carries one notch to everything within reach. · **The Root**: Spend every notch on one creature. The blow lands by the root of its count; nine is the heaviest.
- Gains: Chains through packs by the count; Best single-target finish of his order; Every sigil is his to read. Gives up: Keeps only one Pale Lord, the Witness, who holds the book; No Colossus or Legion; Loses the thread if he misses the beat.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 1/3/0/4. Cousin: Diablo II's Assassin charge-ups with finishers.

### The Walker of the Pale
*Sets white stones, and the sky falls on the lines between them.*

> **A White Stone on a Lowland Road.** Do not move it. Whoever set it will come back to count what lies between it and the next one. On the Ninth Stair the chart of stones grows longer every year, and some of them were set by no warden living.

- Rule broken: The warden comes home. He walks the Pale outward and never turns back.
- Plays: Artillery drawn in lines. He sets white waymarks across the field, and the lines between them become lanes. A Bone Lance thrown along a lane runs its whole length. Bone Rain falls along every lane at once. Pale Lords stand on his stones. Build the lattice, then draw the crowd into it.
- Loop: set stones → draw the lattice → pull the crowd onto the lines → lance along a lane → call the Unfallen down
- Skills: **Pale-Stone**: Set a white waymark, up to nine. A faint chalk line joins each stone to its nearest neighbours. · **Beyond the Pale**: Passive. Anything standing on a line is counted. His bone spells strike the counted first and hardest. · **The Unfallen Asked**: Bone Rain falls straight down along every line at once.
- Gains: Longest reach of any Errant; Lances run whole lanes; Pale Lords on every stone. Gives up: Weak before the stones are set; No melee to speak of; Punished when the crowd will not come to him.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 5/3/1/3. Cousin: a Diablo II Trapsin, with ground drawn in lines.

## The Red Penitent
Nol-Shogthuth, the Bleeding Maiden · draws Vitae · trees: Mortification, Blood, Penance

The rule: The penitent walks in procession, strikes in penance, and gives his blood to her.

### The Nazareno
*Walks the fourteen stations, and cannot stop.*

> **Nazareno's Hood.** The Nazarenos carried the cross all fourteen stations in one night, and were not allowed to set it down between them. The brotherhood kept a list of those who finished. It is a short list. The last name on it is not written in ink.

- Rule broken: The procession walks together. He walks alone, and does not stop.
- Plays: A momentum brawler. Via Crucis becomes the build. Every kill moves him one station along, and each station changes his blows. If he lingers, the road goes cold and he falls back a station. At the fourteenth, his next blow lays down everything near him, and the road begins again.
- Loop: kill → next station → keep moving → falls under the cross at III, VII and IX → the Fourteenth → begin again
- Skills: **The Station**: Passive. Each kill moves him one station along. The station is carved on his orb. · **Fall Under the Cross**: At the third, seventh and ninth stations he falls to his knees in a slam that throws the near ones down. · **The Entombment**: At the fourteenth station his next blow lays down everything around him.
- Gains: Grows stronger every kill; Fastest clear of his order; Big payoff blow. Gives up: No procession: no hoods, no Martyr; Loses stations if he stops; Weak at the start of each area.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 1/5/0/4. Cousin: a Frenzy Barbarian with fourteen stages of momentum.

### The Costalero
*Carries the Maiden through the streets, and the streets kneel.*

> **Costalero's Pad.** In Villa Llaga the costaleros carried the Maiden's paso on their necks: forty men under the boards, blind, keeping step to the foreman's knock. One year the paso came home with forty-one pairs of feet under it. Nobody counted again.

- Rule broken: The penitent gives his blood. He gives his back.
- Plays: A commander of a slow procession. The Sewn Martyr becomes a paso, a great float carried by his hoods with him beneath it. The whole procession moves as one formation around it. His blood spells fall from the paso. What comes near her kneels and is held. He wins by moving the shrine where it needs to be.
- Loop: raise the paso → the hoods take her up → knock: step together → walk her into the crowd → lift her: all near kneel
- Skills: **The Paso**: Raise the float. The Sewn Martyr rides it, and every hood takes a place beneath. · **The Knock**: Three knocks. The procession steps as one, and each step sends a heavy tread through the ground. · **Lift Her**: The paso is raised high. Everything near her kneels and cannot strike.
- Gains: Largest retinue of any Errant; Holds whole crowds in place; Very safe in the middle of it. Gives up: Slow, and moves only as one body; Lost if the hoods are scattered; Little melee of his own.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 3/2/5/1. Cousin: a Diablo II Paladin aura carried on a float.

### The Closed
*Shuts the iron, and lets them tire.*

> **Iron Maiden's Hinge.** A cabinet of iron in the shape of a woman, spikes on the inside of the doors. The brotherhood used it once a year, for one night, and the brother inside was always the one who had asked. They say it was never locked from the outside.

- Rule broken: The penitent strikes in penance. He does not strike at all.
- Plays: A counter-fighter who wins by being struck. He moves between two states. Open, he casts blood. Closed, he walks slowly inside the iron, turns blows back, and every blow he takes draws blood from the striker into pools at his feet. Open the doors when the pools are deep and let it all out at once.
- Loop: close the iron → let them strike → blood pools from every striker → open → the stored blood bursts out
- Skills: **Close the Iron**: Shut the doors. He takes far less, turns blows back, and walks slowly. · **What Is Turned Back**: Passive. Every blow turned back draws blood from its striker into a pool at his feet. · **The Opening**: The doors swing wide. Everything stored in the iron bursts outward.
- Gains: Hardest to kill of any Errant; Harms most where the crowd is thickest; Fills his Vitae from pools. Gives up: No melee string; Weak against foes that stand off; Must choose when to open.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 1/1/1/2. Cousin: Diablo II's Iron Maiden turned into a whole class.

## The Shrine Keeper
The Myriad, the Last Breath · draws Miasma · trees: Miasma, Distortion, Death

The rule: The purifier breathes in, breathes out, and is folded like paper for others.

### The Held Breath
*Stops the clock by not breathing.*

> **The Well-Shrine's Counting Stone.** At the Well-Shrine the children learn to hold their breath under the water and count. Most reach thirty. The purifiers reach a hundred. One girl reached a thousand. The clock over the altar stopped with her, and when she breathed again it had forgotten what hour it was.

- Rule broken: The purifier breathes in and out. She stops.
- Plays: A charge-and-release bomber. While she holds her breath her Miasma swells past its cap, and everything near her moves as if under water. She cannot hold forever: her life thins while she holds, plainly shown on her orb. Let go and it all comes out in one exhale, as large as what she kept.
- Loop: hold → Miasma swells, the world slows around her → wait for the crowd → the long exhale → breathe → hold again
- Skills: **Hold**: Hold. She stops breathing. Miasma swells past its cap, and her life thins while she holds. · **The Still Hour**: Passive. While she holds, things near her move slowly, as if under water. · **The Long Exhale**: Everything held leaves her at once, in a cone as wide as what she kept.
- Gains: The largest single burst in the game; Slows every creature near her; Timing is everything. Gives up: Hurts herself to hold; Little between exhales; A mistimed release is wasted.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 3/2/0/5. Cousin: a charged Blizzard or Meteor release.

### The Unfolded
*She ran out of room, and kept walking.*

> **A Broken Fan-Rib.** Every katashiro ends as an oni. The House says it plainly and early, so that no novice is surprised. It does not say what the oni do after. The Fen's boatmen do: they keep the shrines. Badly, and with great care.

- Rule broken: The doll takes the harm and is burned. She kept it, and became the oni.
- Plays: A shifting brawler. She lets Miasma fill her instead of her cloud, and she grows with it, taking on the oni's frame: poise instead of evasion, and an iron club in place of the fan. At her largest she cannot breathe in at all. She must give it all back to become small and quick again, then start to fill once more.
- Loop: let it in → grow heavy → brawl with the club → full: cannot inhale → give it back in one blast → small and quick again
- Skills: **Let It In**: Miasma fills her instead of the air. Each thing she sickens makes her larger. · **The Iron Club**: Her heavy blow while large. A kanabō of the Fen's shrines, slow and crushing. · **Give It Back**: All of it at once, in one burst around her. She is small, quick and hard to hit again.
- Gains: Two bodies in one build; Great poise while large; Huge burst on the way down. Gives up: No evasion while large; Cannot inhale when full; Clumsy against ranged foes.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 1/3/0/4. Cousin: Diablo II's Druid shapeshifting.

### The Folder of Dolls
*Folds the harm into paper, and sends the paper walking.*

> **Doorstep Doll.** A paper figure on a doorstep asks one to stop. Most do. Those who do not find, a little further on, another figure, a little larger, facing the other way.

- Rule broken: The purifier is the doll. She folds dolls instead.
- Plays: A tactician who is never touched. She spends Omens to fold paper dolls. The dolls walk out, draw blows, and soak up sickness. Write a creature's name on one, and whatever the doll suffers, the creature suffers too. Burn them all together to carry the harm away. The Mirror-Sister fights beside her.
- Loop: fold with Omens → dolls draw the crowd → name the dangerous one → sicken the dolls → the rite of cutting: all burn at once
- Skills: **Fold**: A paper doll walks out of her sleeve and draws blows toward itself. · **Name on the Paper**: Write a creature's name on a doll. Whatever the doll suffers, it suffers. · **The Rite of Cutting**: Every doll burns at once and takes the sickness around it into the fire.
- Gains: Rarely struck; Turns the crowd's attention to paper; Naming makes her deadly one on one too. Gives up: Omens go to dolls, not finishers; Death tree finishers are weaker; Dolls must be set up before fighting.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 4/4/3/2. Cousin: the Amazon's Decoy with PoE curses on it.

## The Empty Hand
Ur-Nihl, the Hush · draws on the hourglass · trees: Radiance, Absence, Destroyer

The rule: The monk carries nothing, keeps nothing, and pays the Silence in sand.

### The Turner of the Glass
*Turns the hourglass with every blow.*

> **The Peak's Hourglass.** The Peak's hourglasses had no top and no bottom. A novice asked which way was up. His teacher turned the glass over and asked him again.

- Rule broken: The sand runs back on its own. He turns the glass himself.
- Plays: A rhythm fighter who alternates his hands. Each Radiance cast strengthens the next Absence cast, and each Absence cast strengthens the next Radiance. Turning the glass swaps his two bulbs, so a nearly full bulb becomes a nearly empty one. When the bulbs are level, both hands work at full strength.
- Loop: light → dark → light → the bulbs level out → both hands full → turn the glass before one chokes
- Skills: **Turn the Glass**: Swap the sand of the two bulbs. · **Noon at Midnight**: Passive. While the bulbs are level, both hands work at full strength. · **The Narrow Waist**: Passive. Each switch from one hand to the other adds weight to the next blow, until he strikes twice with the same hand.
- Gains: Uses both trees at their best; Never locked out of either; The highest skill ceiling in his order. Gives up: Punished for repeating a hand; Little from Destroyer; Needs both trees levelled.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 2/4/0/3. Cousin: a stance dancer like PoE's Blood and Sand.

### The Keeper of Names
*Asks for a name, and does not give it up.*

> **The Begging Bowl.** The bowl has held ten thousand names and still weighs what it weighed when it was carved. The Peak never asked where they went. The lowlanders who whispered their dead into it sometimes dream of a very quiet room, full of people who cannot remember why they are waiting.

- Rule broken: The monk gives every name to the Silence. He keeps them.
- Plays: A controller who turns crowds on themselves. His blows take the struck creature's name. A nameless creature is forgotten by its own kind: they stop following it, stop mending it, and turn on it as a stranger. He holds the names in his bowl, and can empty it at once to give them all to the Silence.
- Loop: ask for names → the nameless are turned on by their own → the bowl fills → empty the bowl
- Skills: **Ask for a Name**: A strike that takes the struck creature's name. A thin grey mark shows where the name was. · **Forgotten**: Passive. The nameless cannot be mended, called or followed by their own kind, who treat them as strangers. · **Empty the Bowl**: Every name he holds is given to the Silence at once. Each nameless creature is erased.
- Gains: Packs fight each other; The strongest crowd control in the game; A massive clearing blow. Gives up: Little raw damage of his own; Weak against lone foes and bosses (bosses keep their names); Sand still costs.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 3/2/0/2. Cousin: Diablo II's Conversion, spread across packs.

### The Stone at the Gate
*Pays with the mountain, never with the glass.*

> **The Weeping Guardian.** At the foot of the Peak's stair stood a guardian of grey stone that wept. When the Order's dead came up the stair, it did not move. The next morning it was found at the top, facing inward, and its tears had run the wrong way.

- Rule broken: The monk pays in sand. He pays with the mountain's own body.
- Plays: A grappler. He sets the hourglass aside and fights only with Destroyer, paid in poise. He seizes creatures, throws them into their own crowd, and bars a line that nothing crosses while he stands on it. His footing is his whole life: staying upright matters more to him than to anyone.
- Loop: take the wrist → throw it into the crowd → step onto the line → bar the stair → take the next
- Skills: **Take the Wrist**: Seize a creature. While he holds it, it shields him. · **The Mountain Thrown**: Throw what he holds. It knocks down whatever it lands on. · **Bar the Stair**: Plant his feet. Nothing crosses the line he stands on while he keeps them planted.
- Gains: Poise is his only cost; The best at holding a doorway; Throws turn one foe into a weapon. Gives up: Radiance and Absence cannot be used; No day and night; Vulnerable once his poise breaks.
- Reach/Motion/Retinue/Risk: 1/2/0/3. Cousin: a grappler; no ARPG has done this well.

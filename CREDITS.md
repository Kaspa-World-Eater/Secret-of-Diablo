# Credits

**Secret of Diablo** is Godmarrow (its code, data, design and music, by Derek) rebuilt in Secret of Mana's
top-down view with free, openly licensed art. None of Godmarrow's own painted art is used.

Much of the art is **CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0**: if you ever share the game or its art, keep this file and the
credit files next to the art, and release derived art under the same licence.

## Characters and monsters — `art/sprites/` (built by `tools/build_lpc_sets.gd`)
- **Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator** layers (bodies, heads, hair, robes, capes, armour, hoods,
  helmets, spears, staves, bows): https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator —
  every original author and licence is listed in that repository's `CREDITS.csv` (CC-BY-SA 3.0, GPL 3.0, OGA-BY 3.0,
  CC-BY 4.0). Layers were recoloured and combined.
- **"[LPC] Monsters"** by Charles Sanchez (CharlesGabriel), bagzie and bluecarrot16 — CC-BY-SA 3.0+ / GPL 3.0+.
  https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-monsters (slime, ghost, worms, bat, eyeball, pumpking, man-eater flower; recoloured)
